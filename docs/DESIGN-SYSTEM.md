# Yunnan Unfolded — Design System

Status: SOURCE OF TRUTH

## Visual direction
Editorial boutique travel. Large photography, generous negative space, disciplined typography and restrained composition. Luxury comes from typography, photography, spacing and restraint—not decorative UI.

## Typography
Use one primary editorial serif and one supporting sans-serif.

Primary editorial serif:
- Used for logo wordmark, hero headlines, major section headings, Journey titles, guide titles and storytelling statements.
- Yunnan and Unfolded must use the same serif family/style in brand applications.
- Hero must not switch Unfolded into an unrelated script/italic font.

Supporting sans-serif:
- Navigation, labels, buttons, metadata, captions, forms and appropriate body/UI copy.

Do not introduce additional decorative font families without approval.

## Color
Use the locked palette from BRAND.md.

## Layout principles
- Large-format photography
- Strong editorial hierarchy
- Generous whitespace
- Restrained navigation and controls
- Avoid SaaS-like card systems
- Avoid excessive rounded corners, shadows, gradients, icons and animation
