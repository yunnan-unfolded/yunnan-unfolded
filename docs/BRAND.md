# Yunnan Unfolded — Brand Guidelines

Status: SOURCE OF TRUTH

## Positioning
Yunnan Unfolded is a boutique, locally rooted Yunnan travel brand for international travelers seeking thoughtful, slow and highly personalized journeys. It combines the sensibility of an independent travel magazine, a boutique travel studio and genuine local Yunnan expertise.

It is NOT a mass-market tour operator, backpacker booking site, generic China travel agency or outdoor-sports company.

Core themes: hiking, nature, culture, villages, food & tea, photography, local people, discovery and tailor-made journeys.

## Brand character
Quiet, sophisticated, curious, warm, editorial, nature-led, culturally respectful, premium without being flashy.

## Logo — LOCKED
Direction: Mountain Fold.
- Minimal line-art mountain mark
- Small Tea Gold sun
- Yunnan / Unfolded wordmark
- Yunnan and Unfolded use the SAME serif typeface/style
- No script treatment for Unfolded
- Keep generous breathing room between mountain mark and wordmark
- Do not redraw or casually reinterpret the logo

Logo system supports primary stacked, horizontal, wordmark-only and icon-only versions.

## Palette — LOCKED
- Forest Green: #14362E
- Sage Green: #6F7F6A
- Warm Ivory: #F7F3EB
- Tea Gold: #C9A46A
- Charcoal: #222222

Avoid gradients, bright colors, heavy shadows and unnecessary effects.

## Founder
Chloe is the founder/local voice. Position her as a knowledgeable local travel professional and passionate hiker, not as an influencer. Founder storytelling should communicate firsthand knowledge, curiosity and access to quieter routes, people and stories.

## Photography
Use authentic Yunnan imagery wherever possible: mountains, trails, villages, terraces, tea landscapes, local people, food, culture, hikers and contemplative human moments. Avoid generic alpine imagery that is visibly not Yunnan and avoid mass-tourism stock aesthetics.
